Research

M.Sc. thesis project

Surface charge-tailored paclitaxel nanocrystals for intravenous delivery

Paredes Lab, School of Pharmacy, Queen's University Belfast · April – September 2026

I produced paclitaxel nanocrystals by wet-media milling, stabilised with Poloxamer 407 plus either Eudragit E100 (cationic) or sodium dodecyl sulphate (anionic), so the two formulations could be compared by surface charge.
